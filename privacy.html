Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, games, and related services ("Applications") published under our developer account. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Our Applications do not directly collect or store personal identification information (such as names, physical addresses, or user account credentials). However, integrated third-party frameworks may process non-personal diagnostic and usage metrics:

  • Device & Identifier Data: Advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP address, device type, operating system version, and system language.
  • In-App Purchase Data: Transaction validation status, purchase history, and active digital item entitlements.
  • Gameplay & Analytics: Application crash logs, session duration, and performance metrics.

2. Third-Party Services & Frameworks

Our Applications integrate third-party SDKs to provide advertisements, manage digital purchases, and ensure operational stability. Each service handles data according to its respective privacy standards:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Ad serving and mobile service infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat: In-app purchase validation, subscription management, and entitlement tracking.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: Secure payment processing and storefront services.

3. How We Use Information

Data processed by third-party services is used strictly to:

  • Deliver, restore, and validate in-app purchases across user sessions.
  • Serve contextual or personalized advertisements where permitted.
  • Analyze technical stability, resolve crashes, and improve overall user experience.

4. Data Protection & Security

We do not maintain external private database servers to store user identities or sensitive data. Security and storage of third-party operational data are handled directly by our compliant partners (Google, Apple, RevenueCat).

5. Children's Privacy

Our Applications are designed to comply with applicable privacy regulations regarding children. We do not knowingly gather personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe such data has been transmitted inadvertently via third-party services, please contact us for immediate resolution.

6. User Controls & Contact Information

Users may manage tracking permissions or reset advertising identifiers at any time via native device settings (iOS Settings > Privacy / Android Settings > Privacy/Google).
